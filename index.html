Who We Are

Pack 96 is a family-centered Cub Scout pack chartered by St. Michael School in Sharonville and part of the Dan Beard Council of Scouting America. We welcome all children in kindergarten through 5th grade.

Through den meetings, pack events, service projects, campouts, and long-standing traditions like the Pinewood Derby and the Blue & Gold Banquet, Scouts grow in character, citizenship, and confidence while making lasting memories.

What is Cub Scouting?

Cub Scouting is the Scouting America program for youth in kindergarten through 5th grade. The program develops:

  • Character — integrity, honesty, good citizenship
  • Outdoor skills — camping, hiking, nature
  • STEM — hands-on science and engineering
  • Leadership — teamwork, responsibility, confidence
  • Service — helping our community

How It Works

Pack 96 serves children from kindergarten through fifth grade, organized into grade-based dens where they meet with other Scouts their age to work on adventures, build friendships, and learn new skills. Den meetings may include games, crafts, outdoor activities, service, science projects, citizenship lessons, and advancement work.

The Pack (all grades) meets roughly once a month throughout the school year, usually on the third Friday. Each den (one grade level) meets one to three times per month depending on the level. Parents attend den meetings with their Scout in kindergarten through 2nd grade.

Dues & Fundraising

Pack dues cover national registration, the council fee, the pack T-shirt, a Pinewood Derby kit, badges, campout facilities, and event supplies. Fundraisers through the year, like the Krispy Kreme doughnut sale and dine-and-donate nights, help keep dues down. Financial assistance is available: no Scout is ever turned away.

Our Dens

Cub Scouts grow through grade-based adventures that build confidence, character, citizenship, safety awareness, outdoor skills, and family involvement. Each rank includes required and elective Adventures that help Scouts learn by doing. Den leaders share exact meeting schedules and activity details as the year progresses.

Webelos

4th & 5th Grade

At Pack 96, our older Cub Scouts continue together in a two-year Webelos experience focused on growing independence, leadership, outdoor skills, service, and preparation for the next stage of Scouting. Scouting America officially separates Webelos and Arrow of Light for advancement, but Pack 96 keeps older Cub Scouts together in a shared local experience. Webelos bridge into Troop 803 at St. Michael.

The Scout Oath

“On my honor I will do my best
to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law;
to help other people at all times;
to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.”

The Scout Law

A Scout is trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent.

And the Cub Scout motto: “Do Your Best.”

  • Chaperone a campout
  • Lead or assist a den, or lead one den meeting activity
  • Help with a fundraiser or event setup and cleanup
  • Share a skill, hobby, or career with the Scouts
  • Serve on the pack committee

Talk to your den leader or any committee member.
